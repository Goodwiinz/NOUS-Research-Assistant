## Proven

| acceptance box | evidence | result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedules survive a worker restart; retained execution evidence | `scenario-crash.json`, `timeline.json` | The worker's process group was SIGKILLed at 07:09:19 UTC, within the same second its `started` attempt was recorded. The execution, its attempt and the schedule stayed in the database; a new worker started. For 200 s (more than three ticks) no second run began. 30 min 0.03 s after the first attempt a new worker added `started` with `retry_of` the first attempt's id, then `succeeded` (same classes as the main fire). Two workers appear in the attempts (`…:48682`, then `…:55389`), and the killed worker process exited with -9 |
| Retry cannot double-count | `scenario-crash.json` | One execution and one import receipt (`scheduled:<execution_id>`) after the retry |
| Duplicate and overlapping ticks do not duplicate executions | `log-summary.json`, `scenario-main.json` | Two beats ticked together: 41 and 37 ticks sent, 78 received by the three workers. Each of the four schedules has exactly one execution; the main one has `started`, `succeeded` |
| Delta resolves to strategy, execution and both corpus snapshots | `delta-main.json`, `snapshots-main.json` | The sealed `nous.academic.search-delta.v1` export names the schedule version (cron `5 * * * *`, `Europe/London`, pinned strategy), execution, import receipt, baseline snapshot digest `7e7e7da5…` (no earlier execution) and post-fire snapshot digest `507fda31…`. Two reads give the same body hash |
| New, changed, corrected or retracted, unchanged and unknown are distinguishable | `scenario-main.json` | Live providers returned 29 works against a 4-work baseline: `new` 27, `changed` 1 (baseline year raised by one), `unchanged` 1, `corrected_retracted` 1, `unknown` 1 |
